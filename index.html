AI help on every job

A cover letter and a sharper CV, for each job.

Press AI help on any job, in your results, your feed or your shortlist. It reads the full listing and your CV, writes the letter, and shows you how to reword your CV for that job.

Cover letter
Four short paragraphs that answer the listing's must-haves with real lines from your CV. Edit it, copy it, send it.
CV tips
Rewrites that borrow the listing's own wording, the keywords to add where they're true, and honest ways to cover a gap.
Your words
Nothing invented: it only uses what your CV says, in English, French or the listing's own language.
