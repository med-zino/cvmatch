Privacy policy

Last updated 15 September 2026

This page explains what Pounce stores about you, why, who helps us run the service, and how to have your data deleted.

What we store

  • Your account: your name, your email address and a hashed password. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account ID instead of a password.
  • Your CV: the text of the CV you upload or paste, and its file name, so you only add it once. We don't keep the file itself.
  • Your job hunt: the job titles and city you choose, your searches, the jobs in your feed and their scores, the jobs you save with their status and notes, and the cover letters and CV tips you generate.
  • Your email settings: whether the daily matches email is on, the time you picked and your time zone.

Why we use it

  • To run the service you signed up for: searching for jobs, scoring them against your CV, writing letters and tips, and keeping your shortlist.
  • To send the emails you need or ask for: the email that confirms your address and, if you turn it on, the daily matches email.

We never sell your data, never show your CV to employers and never use it for advertising.

Who helps us run Pounce

  • Google Gemini API reads your CV and the job listings to score jobs and to write cover letters and CV tips. Pounce currently uses the free tier of this API. Under Google's terms for that tier, Google may use what is sent to it to improve its products, and its reviewers may read it.
  • RapidAPI (JSearch) receives the job title and city you search for, and returns listings. It never receives your CV.
  • MongoDB Atlas hosts our database.
  • Vercel hosts the website and counts page visits without cookies or personal data.
  • Google handles sign-in if you choose Sign in with Google, and Gmail sends our emails.

Some of these providers process data outside your country, including in the United States.

Cookies and local storage

Pounce sets one cookie, which keeps you signed in for 24 hours. Your browser also keeps a few small settings, such as whether you've seen the tour. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your saved CV at any time with Delete saved CV on the Find matches page. Your feed only keeps your most recent 400 jobs. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete it and everything linked to it within one month.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, and object to how we use it. If you live in the EU or the UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).
